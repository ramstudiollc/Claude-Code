### 5.3 New automated rules (so the problems can't creep back)

Human problem + takeaway on every post · open loop + payoff on every Reel, with payoff times inside the runtime · banned generic hook openers · ≤ 1 hook ending in "here's why/how" · hard service CTAs only on SVC posts, ≤ 1/day, ≤ 14 total · soft service lines ≤ 8 · no caption ending or final Reel line used more than twice · ≤ 8 prompt-card images · no image or Reel format on 3+ consecutive days · weekday words must match the posting day · similar-takeaway detection · extra banned phrases ("you won't believe", "this changes everything", "unlock your potential").
