**Caption**

```text
Stop zooming in on the hands. New AI images get them right. Check the source instead:

1. Long-press the image → search with Google Lens → look for the earliest result
2. Is any trusted news outlet showing the same thing?
3. Check the date. Many “new” fakes are real photos from years ago.
4. Look for an “AI info” label. Meta adds it when it detects AI signals or the poster discloses it.

No label doesn't prove it's real. Can't trace it? Don't share it.

Send this to the group that shares everything.

#factcheck #AIliteracy #deepfake
```
**Production notes:** Generate your own example image and keep an 'AI-generated example' label on it the whole time. Never use a real viral fake (it spreads it further).
