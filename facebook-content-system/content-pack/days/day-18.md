- **The idea:** A 5-step quick check (exact claim, two known sources, dates, agreement, don't repeat if only AI says it).
- **What they leave with:** Check an AI claim against two known, dated sources before you repeat it.
- **Call to action:** Share: Send it to a group where people forward everything.

- Headline: Check an AI claim in 60 seconds

  - 2. Search it, and open 2 sources you know.

  - 5. Only AI says it? Don't repeat it.

## 21:00 · Reel · D18-R2: 5 questions to ask before hiring an automation freelancer

**Series:** Work With Me · **Content type:** Work with me (n8n services) · **Format:** Numbered list + talking head · **Length:** 48 s

- **The problem it solves:** Paying someone to automate, then being stuck when they disappear or it breaks.
- **The idea:** Ownership, failure handling, test data, monthly cost and handover separate professionals from risky builds.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** A freelancer builds your automation, then disappears.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** What do I ask before hiring so that doesn't happen to me?
- **Where it pays off:** 3–33 s: five questions to ask; 33–39 s: the red flag to walk away from.
- **What they leave with:** Before hiring an automation builder, ask about account ownership, alerts, test data, running costs and a handover guide.
- **Call to action:** Message me (service): Message 'AUTOMATE' and ask all five.
- **Overall look:** Numbered question cards; a red-flag card; Asif inviting the questions.

- Usefulness: Five concrete questions anyone can ask any freelancer.

- Visual progression: A new numbered card every ~5 s, then a red-flag reveal.
