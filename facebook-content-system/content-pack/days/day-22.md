## 18:30 · Image · D22-I2: Posting AI images or voice? When to label them

**Series:** AI Myth Check · **Content type:** AI literacy & safety · **Format:** Three-tier card

- **The problem it solves:** Posting AI-made images or voice without knowing when Facebook expects a label.
- **The idea:** Meta requires disclosure for photorealistic AI video and realistic audio, and may auto-label; labelling realistic AI images is a good habit; faking real people is never OK.
- **What they leave with:** Disclose photorealistic AI video and realistic audio, label realistic AI images anyway, and never fake real people.
- **Call to action:** No CTA (the takeaway is the ending): None. The closing line is the takeaway.

**Text on the image** (use exactly)

- Headline: Posting AI images or voice? When to label them
- Body:
  - Must disclose: photorealistic video or realistic audio made or changed with AI.
  - Use Meta's AI disclosure option when you post.
  - Meta may also add an 'AI info' label automatically.
  - Good habit: write 'AI-generated' on any realistic AI image.
  - Never: fake a real person saying or doing something.
- Footer: Source: Meta's AI labelling policy (2024), checked Oct 2026.

**What the image shows:** Must / Should / Never three-tier card with a small 'AI info' tag icon.

**Layout idea** (your brand guidelines decide colours and fonts): Light background. Three bands: green 'MUST', blue 'GOOD HABIT', red 'NEVER', with rows inside. Small 'AI info' tag illustration top-right. Footer small.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text

Must: Meta requires you to use its AI disclosure option when you post photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was made or changed with AI.
```
