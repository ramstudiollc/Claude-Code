## 13:30 · Reel · D03-R1: Show, don't describe: paste examples to get your style

**Series:** Before → After · **Content type:** Practical AI · **Format:** Before/after split screen · **Length:** 35 s

- **The problem it solves:** AI-written posts and emails that sound nothing like you.
- **The idea:** Adjectives like 'friendly but professional' are guesses; 2–3 real examples make AI match your style.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** Sounds nothing like you? Stop describing your style.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** If describing your style doesn't work, what does?
- **Where it pays off:** 10.5–24 s: pasting 2–3 of your own posts gives a draft with your length, words and sign-off.
- **What they leave with:** Paste examples of your own writing instead of describing the style you want.
- **Call to action:** Save: Save it for your next caption or email.
- **Overall look:** Split screen: left = adjective prompt + stiff output; right = pasted examples + matching output with features circled.

**Video script** (times in seconds; say the voiceover word for word)

| Time | Voiceover (say) | On-screen text | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Sounds nothing like you? Stop describing your style. | AI doesn't sound like you? | Asif to camera; screen splits into two halves. |
| 3-7 | Most people type: “Make it friendly but professional, short, with energy.” | “friendly but professional…” | Left half: adjective prompt typed; output appears. |
| 7-10.5 | AI reads those words a hundred different ways. | adjectives = guesses | Left output: stiff phrases highlighted in yellow. |
| 10.5-15 | Instead, paste two or three things you already wrote and liked. | Paste 2–3 of YOUR examples | Right half: two old posts pasted in. |
| 15-19.5 | Then say: “Write the new one in the same style as these.” | “Same style as these.” | Right half: the instruction typed. |
| 19.5-24 | Look. Same sentence length, same words you use, same sign-off. | your length · your words · your sign-off | Right output appears; three matching features circled. |
| 24-28 | It works for emails, captions, product descriptions, even reports. | emails · captions · products · reports | Quick montage of four document types. |

| 32-35 | Because AI copies everything, including your mistakes. | It copies mistakes too | Small smile; end frame. |

**Why people keep watching**
- Hook: A direct instruction that contradicts how most people prompt ('stop describing').
- Problem → solution: Shows the stiff result first, then the fix, inside 20 seconds.
- Visual progression: Left/right split fills in step by step; matching features get circled on the right.
- Payoff: The final output visibly matches the user's own sentence length, words and sign-off.
- Usefulness: Works for emails, captions, product descriptions and reports, with the prompt in the caption.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
AI never sounds like you? Stop describing your style. Paste it.

“Friendly but professional” can mean 100 things to AI. Your own examples mean one thing.

Try:
“Here are 3 posts I wrote and liked: [paste]. Write a new post about [topic] in the same style: same length, same tone, same sign-off.”

Only paste examples you'd sign. AI copies the mistakes too.

Save this for your next caption or email.

#AItips #ChatGPT #copywriting
```

## 18:30 · Image · D03-I2: ChatGPT Temporary Chat: when to use it

**Series:** Hidden Button · **Content type:** Hidden features & tools · **Format:** Hidden feature card

- **The problem it solves:** Wanting to ask AI something without it staying in your history or changing what it remembers.
- **The idea:** Temporary Chat keeps a conversation out of history and memory; useful for one-offs, tests and screen-sharing.
- **What they leave with:** Temporary Chat keeps a conversation out of ChatGPT's history and memory, but it is not for secrets.
- **Call to action:** No CTA (the takeaway is the ending): None. The privacy reminder is the closing line.

**Text on the image** (use exactly)

- Headline: Don't want this chat saved? Use Temporary Chat
- Body:
  - One-off questions you don't want in your history.
  - Testing a prompt without changing ChatGPT's memory of you.
  - Sharing your screen without old chats showing.
  - New (Aug 2026): choose personalized or unpersonalized before you start.
  - It never saves new memories. Still no passwords or ID numbers.
- Footer: Source: OpenAI Help Center, checked Oct 2026

**What the image shows:** Simple UI-style mock of a chat window with a dotted 'temporary' outline (drawn, not a screenshot), four use cases beside it.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text

Good for:
• one-off questions you don't want in your history
• testing a prompt without changing what ChatGPT remembers about you
• sharing your screen without old chats showing
```
