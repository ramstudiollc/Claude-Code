- **What they leave with:** Practise interviews out loud in AI voice mode, then ask for feedback on your weakest answer.
- **Call to action:** No CTA (the takeaway is the ending): None. 'Practise tonight' is the action.
- **Overall look:** Phone propped like a video call; Asif answering out loud; feedback text on screen.

**Caption** (copy and paste)
