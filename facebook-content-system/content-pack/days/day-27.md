**Series:** Steal This Prompt · **Content type:** Workflows & productivity · **Format:** Framework card

- **The problem it solves:** Writing long captions that nobody reads past the first line.
- **The idea:** Hook under ~110 characters, a useful middle, one clear ask; AI can draft hook options.
- **What they leave with:** Put the hook in the first 110 characters, the useful part in the middle and one clear ask at the end.
- **Call to action:** No CTA (the takeaway is the ending): None. Ends by pointing out that this page uses the same structure.

**Text on the image** (use exactly)

- Headline: A Facebook caption structure that gets read
- Body:
  - Line 1: the hook. What they get, under 110 characters.
  - Middle: the useful part. Steps, a prompt, or the key fact.
  - Last: one clear ask. Save, send, or message. Not all three.
  - Prompt: “Write 3 hooks under 110 characters for this post: [paste].”
- Footer: Facebook shows about 125 characters before 'See more'.
