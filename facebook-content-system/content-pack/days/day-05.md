## 13:30 · Reel · D05-R1: 5 things you should never paste into AI

**Series:** AI Myth Check · **Content type:** AI literacy & safety · **Format:** Numbered list + talking head · **Length:** 37 s

- **The problem it solves:** Pasting private or sensitive data into AI chats without thinking about where it goes.
- **The idea:** Five categories of data don't belong in AI chats; placeholders keep the help without the risk.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** You've probably pasted one of these into AI.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** Which of the five have I already done?
- **Where it pays off:** 25–31 s: placeholders let you get the same help without sharing the data.
- **What they leave with:** Replace private details with placeholders before you paste anything into AI.
- **Call to action:** Question to the viewer: Ask viewers which one they've pasted before.
- **Overall look:** Numbered cards slide in beside Asif; final screen recording shows names swapped for [CLIENT] placeholders.

**Video script** (times in seconds; say the voiceover word for word)

| Time | Voiceover (say) | On-screen text | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | You've probably pasted one of these into AI. | Pasted one of these? | Asif to camera; hand covers a phone screen. |
| 3-7 | One: passwords and OTP codes. No tool ever needs them. | 1 · Passwords & OTPs | Card 1 slides in. |
| 7-11 | Two: card numbers and mobile banking PINs. | 2 · Card numbers & PINs | Card 2 slides in. |
| 11-15 | Three: your NID or passport number, or anyone else's. | 3 · NID & passport numbers | Card 3 slides in. |
| 15-20 | Four: customer phone lists or client files, without their permission. | 4 · Other people's data | Card 4 slides in. |
| 20-25 | Five: anything under an NDA, or a contract you promised to keep private. | 5 · Anything under NDA | Card 5 slides in. |
| 25-31 | Need help with a real document? Swap details for placeholders: client A, amount X. | Use [CLIENT] · [AMOUNT] | Screen recording: names and amounts replaced by [CLIENT], [AMOUNT]. |
| 31-37 | You still get the help, without the risk. Which one have you pasted before? | Which one have you pasted? | Asif to camera; end card. |

**Why people keep watching**
- Hook: 'You've probably done this' makes every viewer check themselves against the list.
- Relatability: Mobile-banking PINs, NID numbers and customer phone lists are everyday data for this audience.
- Visual progression: A new numbered card every 4–5 seconds keeps the screen changing.
- Usefulness: The placeholder trick lets people keep using AI on real work safely.
- Saves and shares: Teams forward it; the honest question invites comments without bait.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
You've probably pasted at least one of these into an AI chat:

1. Passwords and OTP codes
2. Card numbers and mobile-banking PINs
3. NID or passport numbers (yours or anyone's)
4. Customer phone lists or client files, without permission
5. Anything under an NDA or confidentiality promise

Need help with a real document? Replace the details with placeholders: [CLIENT], [AMOUNT], [PHONE]. You still get the help; the AI never sees the data.

Be honest: which one have you pasted before? (No judgement.)

#AIsafety #privacy #AItips
```

**Notes:** Use a fake document for the placeholder demo. Don't show any real customer data, even blurred.

## 21:00 · Reel · D05-R2: Spreadsheet formulas from plain English, plus the 3-row test

**Series:** Steal This Prompt · **Content type:** Practical AI · **Format:** Screen demo + face cam · **Length:** 44 s

- **The problem it solves:** Needing a total from a spreadsheet but not knowing the formula.
- **The idea:** Describe the goal, columns and rows; get a formula with an explanation; always test on rows you can check by hand.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** Can't write Excel formulas? Describe them instead.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** What's the part most people skip? (held until 17.5 s)
- **Where it pays off:** 21–31 s: the 3-row test proves the formula before it touches 500 rows.
- **What they leave with:** Describe the result you need and test the AI's formula on rows you can check by hand.
- **Call to action:** Save: Save it for your next spreadsheet.
- **Overall look:** Google Sheet order list; AI prompt and answer; 3 highlighted rows with hand math overlay; green tick.

**Video script** (times in seconds; say the voiceover word for word)

| Time | Voiceover (say) | On-screen text | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Can't write Excel formulas? Describe them instead. | Describe it. Don't memorise it. | Asif face; a messy sheet behind him. |
| 3-7.5 | Here's my order sheet: date, amount, status. I want October's delivered sales. | Goal: Oct delivered sales | Screen: sheet with columns A Date, B Amount, C Status. |
| 7.5-13 | So I tell AI exactly that, plus the column letters and where data starts. | Say: goal + columns + rows | Prompt typed (same as caption). |
| 13-17.5 | It gives a formula, and explains each part in plain words. | Formula + explanation | AI answer with SUMIFS formula; explanation lines highlighted. |
| 17.5-21 | Now the important part most people skip. | The part people skip ↓ | Asif leans in. |
| 21-26.5 | Test it on three rows you can check by hand. Here, I expect 4,500. | Test on 3 rows | 3-row test copy highlighted; overlay 1,200 + 2,000 + 1,300 = 4,500. |
| 26.5-31 | It says 4,500. Now I can trust all 500 rows. | ✓ 4,500 = 4,500 | Cell shows 4,500; green tick animation. |
| 31-36.5 | If it's wrong, paste the error or the wrong result back. It fixes it. | Wrong? Paste it back | Screen: error pasted into chat; corrected formula returned. |
| 36.5-41 | Works in Google Sheets and Excel. The prompt is in the caption. | Prompt in caption ↓ | Asif points down. |
| 41-44 | Save it for your next spreadsheet. | Save for later | End card. |

**Why people keep watching**
- Hook: Speaks to a common insecurity (formulas) and removes it in one line.
- Problem → solution: A real sales question goes from blank to answered on screen.
- Curiosity: 'The part most people skip' at second 17 holds viewers for the twist.
- Payoff: The formula result matches the hand-checked 4,500 with a green tick.
- Saves and shares: Prompt template in the caption is reusable for any spreadsheet question.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
Can't write spreadsheet formulas? Describe them instead.

Prompt:
“I have a sheet. Column A = order date, B = amount, C = status. Data is in rows 2–500. Write a Google Sheets formula for the total amount of orders with status ‘Delivered’ in October 2026. Explain each part in one line.”

Then the step most people skip: test it on 3 rows where you already know the answer. If it matches, use it on the full sheet. If not, paste the wrong result back and ask it to fix the formula.

Works for Excel too. Just say “Excel” instead.

The 3-row test is the habit worth keeping. It works for any formula.

#GoogleSheets #Excel #AItips
```

**Notes:** Build a demo sheet with fake orders. Make a separate 3-row test tab where the known total is 4,500 (1,200 + 2,000 + 1,300, all Delivered, all October). Record the real AI answer; if the formula differs from SUMIFS, show what you actually got.
