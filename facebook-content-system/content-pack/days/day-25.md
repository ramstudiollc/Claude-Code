## 13:30 · Reel · D25-R1: Why long AI chats get worse (and the clean-desk fix)

**Series:** AI Myth Check · **Content type:** AI literacy & safety · **Format:** Prop explainer + talking head · **Length:** 37 s

- **The problem it solves:** Long AI chats where it slowly forgets the rules you set at the start.
- **The idea:** AI reads chats in tokens within a limit; as a chat grows, early instructions get buried or dropped; summarise and restart.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** Twenty minutes in, AI forgot my rules.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** Why does it forget, and how do I stop it?
- **Where it pays off:** 8.5–32 s: the full-desk explanation, then summarise-and-restart.
- **What they leave with:** When a long chat starts forgetting, ask for a summary and continue in a fresh chat.
- **Call to action:** Save: Save it.
- **Overall look:** Real desk + real paper: Asif stacks printed 'messages' on a small desk until the first sheets slide off; then writes a summary card and moves it to a clean desk. Short screen inserts for the prompt.

|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Twenty minutes in, AI forgot my rules. | AI forgot my rules | Asif scrolling a very long chat. |

| 8.5-13 | Think of it as a desk. Every message adds paper. | Chat = a desk | Real desk: Asif adds printed message sheets one by one. |

**Why people keep watching**
- Hook: A personal, familiar moment (AI ignoring rules you gave it earlier) rather than a technical statement.
- Curiosity: 'Here's why' sets up a simple reveal (tokens and a limit).
- Visual progression: Paper-on-a-desk animation makes an invisible limit visible.
- Usefulness: A one-prompt fix viewers can use immediately.
- Novelty: Introduces 'tokens' and 'context limits' in plain language.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
AI forgot the instructions you gave it 20 minutes ago? It's not your imagination.

AI reads your chat in small pieces called tokens, and every chat has a limit. Think of a desk: every message adds paper. When it's full, older papers get buried or fall off, and often the first instructions you gave are the ones it forgets.

Fix:
1. “Summarise the goal, the decisions so far and my preferences in 10 bullets.”
2. Start a new chat and paste the summary.
3. Rules you always want → save them as custom instructions (or a skill).

Next time an answer suddenly ignores your rules, start fresh with a summary.

#AItips #ChatGPT #AIliteracy
```

**Notes:** Desk-and-paper motion template. Keep the claim general (no exact token limits; they differ by model).
