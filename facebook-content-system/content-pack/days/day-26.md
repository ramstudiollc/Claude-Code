## 09:30 · Image · D26-I1: Stuck on an error? Paste this, not just the error

**Series:** Steal This Prompt · **Content type:** Practical AI · **Format:** Prompt card

- **The problem it solves:** Pasting an error into AI and getting ten random fixes that don't work.
- **The idea:** Error text + what you did + what you expected + what you tried gets a far better fix than the error alone.
- **What they leave with:** Give AI the error, what you did, what you expected and what you tried, and ask for one fix first.
- **Call to action:** Save: Save it.

**Text on the image** (use exactly)

- Headline: Stuck on an error? Paste this, not just the error
- Body:
  - “Here's the error: [exact text or screenshot].
  - What I was doing: [steps].
  - What I expected: [result].
  - What I already tried: [list].
  - Explain the cause simply, then give me one fix to try first.”
- Footer: Works for Excel, WordPress, phones, printers and n8n.

**What the image shows:** Red error dialog icon + four-part template card.

**Layout idea** (your brand guidelines decide colours and fonts): Light background. Red error-dialog icon top-left. Template as a quote block with bracketed fields in accent colour. Footer bottom.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
Pasting only the error message gets you a list of 10 random fixes. Paste this instead:

“Here's the error: [exact text or screenshot].
What I was doing: [steps].
What I expected: [result].
What I already tried: [list].
Explain the cause in simple words, then give me ONE fix to try first.”

“One fix first” is the key. You test, report back, and it narrows down.

Works for Excel, WordPress, phones, printers and n8n.

#techtips #AItips #troubleshooting
```

## 13:30 · Reel · D26-R1: The 10-minute Friday review with AI

**Series:** Steal This Prompt · **Content type:** Workflows & productivity · **Format:** Talking head + screen split · **Length:** 40 s

- **The problem it solves:** Feeling busy all week but being unable to say what actually got done.
- **The idea:** Paste done/not done/calendar, ask four questions (finished, slipping, stop, top 3), then choose one repeated task to automate, delegate or delete.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** Busy all week. Finished what, exactly?
- **What the viewer wants to know:** Which four questions make the week clear?
- **Where it pays off:** 7.5–32.5 s: finished, slipping, stop and top three, then one task to automate, delegate or delete.
- **What they leave with:** Spend ten minutes each Friday asking AI what you finished, what keeps slipping, what to stop and your top three.
- **Call to action:** Question to the viewer: Ask what keeps slipping for viewers every week.
- **Overall look:** Friday evening tea + laptop; AI answers to four questions; callback to the Day 1 decision tree.

## 18:30 · Image · D26-I2: 5 mistakes I see in DIY automations

**Series:** Work With Me · **Content type:** Work with me (n8n services) · **Format:** Checklist

- **The problem it solves:** DIY automations that quietly fail, duplicate data or let AI make risky changes.
- **The idea:** No alerts, testing on customers, giant workflows, no duplicate checks and unsupervised AI money decisions cause most DIY failures.
- **What they leave with:** Add error alerts, test with fake data, split big workflows, check for duplicates and keep humans on money decisions.
- **Call to action:** Question to the viewer: Ask which mistake is in the viewer's workflow right now.

**Text on the image** (use exactly)

- Headline: 5 mistakes I see in DIY automations
- Body:
  - No error alert: it fails quietly for days.
  - Testing on real customers.
  - One giant workflow doing ten jobs. Hard to fix.
  - No duplicate check: orders counted twice.
  - AI allowed to change prices or send refunds alone.
- Footer: Built it yourself? Fix these first.

**What the image shows:** Five red warning rows with small illustrations.

**Layout idea** (your brand guidelines decide colours and fonts): Dark background. Five rows each with a red warning icon and a tiny illustration. Footer as a short bold line at the bottom.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
Built your own automation? Check for these 5 mistakes:

1. No error alert: it fails quietly for days
2. Testing on real customers instead of fake data
3. One giant workflow doing ten jobs, so it's hard to fix
4. No duplicate check, so orders or rows get counted twice
5. AI allowed to change prices or send refunds without a human

Fix these first and most problems disappear.

Which of these is in your workflow right now?

#n8n #automation #nocode
```

**Notes:** Warning-list template. 'Mistakes I see' should reflect Asif's real experience; edit any item you haven't seen.

## 21:00 · Reel · D26-R2: What I need from you to automate your business (just 4 things)

**Series:** Work With Me · **Content type:** Work with me (n8n services) · **Format:** Talking head + b-roll · **Length:** 45 s

- **The problem it solves:** Owners assume they need technical knowledge or perfect documentation before asking for automation.
- **The idea:** A voice-note description, a screen recording, app names (not passwords) and 3–5 examples including a weird one are enough to start.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** Not technical? I just need four things.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** What are the four things?
- **Where it pays off:** 3–34 s: a voice note, a screen recording, the app names and real examples, including a weird one.
- **What they leave with:** To start an automation, share the task in your words, a screen recording, the app names and a few real examples.
- **Call to action:** Message me (service): Message 'AUTOMATE' and send the voice note first.
- **Overall look:** Talking head with four icons appearing (voice note, phone recording, app icons, example cards).

**Video script** (times in seconds; say the voiceover word for word)

| Time | Voiceover (say) | On-screen text | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Not technical? I just need four things. | Not technical? 4 things. | Asif to camera. |
| 3-8 | One: the task in your own words. A voice note is fine. | 1 · The task, in your words | Voice-note icon. |
| 8-13.5 | Two: a screen recording of you doing it once. Your phone is enough. | 2 · Record yourself doing it | Phone screen-recording icon. |
| 13.5-18.5 | That recording shows me steps you'd never think to mention. | Shows hidden steps | B-roll of a screen recording with a step highlighted. |
| 18.5-23.5 | Three: which apps are involved. Not passwords yet. Just the names. | 3 · App names (no passwords) | App icons. |

- Hook: Removes the biggest fear (not being technical) in the first sentence.

- Usefulness: Clear, doable preparation steps.
- Novelty: Asking for a screen recording and a 'weird' example is unusual and shows expertise.
- Payoff: Ends with a single easy action: send a voice note.

```text
Have a repetitive task you want to automate? Message me “AUTOMATE” and send the voice note first.
```
