## 21:00 · Reel · D16-R2: The 4-part prompt: goal, context, example, format

- **The problem it solves:** Typing a one-line request and getting a bland result back.

- **Hook (first 3 s):** Good prompts have four parts. Yours has one.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** What are the other three parts?
- **Where it pays off:** 31–40 s: the before/after shows what the four-part prompt produces.
- **What they leave with:** Add a goal, context, an example and a format to a request to get much better AI results.
- **Call to action:** Save: Save it.
- **Overall look:** Text-led: kinetic typography on a plain background. Four letter blocks G, C, E, F fill one by one; short screen inserts for the before/after. Asif's voice from frame 1.

```text
Most prompts are just a task: “Write a post about my discount.” Good prompts have 4 parts:

G: Goal: what's it for and who reads it? “Get office workers in Gulshan to pre-order lunch by 11 AM.”
```
