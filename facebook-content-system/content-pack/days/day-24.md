## 18:30 · Image · D24-I2: 3 rules that beat almost every AI scam

**Series:** AI Myth Check · **Content type:** AI literacy & safety · **Format:** Three-rule card

- **The problem it solves:** AI-powered scams that sound and look more real every month.
- **The idea:** Second-channel verification, treating urgency as a red flag, and never sharing OTPs/PINs defeat voice, video and text scams.
- **What they leave with:** Verify on a second channel, treat urgency as a red flag and never share OTPs or PINs.
- **Call to action:** Share: Send it to someone who'd panic first and check later.

**Text on the image** (use exactly)

- Headline: 3 rules that beat almost every AI scam
- Body:
  - 1. Verify on a second channel. Call back on a number you saved.
  - 2. Urgency is the trick. Real people can wait 5 minutes.
  - 3. Never share OTPs or PINs. Real companies don't ask for them.
  - Fake voices, fake videos, perfect-English messages: same 3 rules.
- Footer: Send to someone who'd panic first and check later.

**What the image shows:** Three large numbered shields.

**Layout idea** (your brand guidelines decide colours and fonts): Dark background. Three large shield icons in a row with numbers; rules underneath each. Summary line below. Footer bottom.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
AI scams keep changing: cloned voices, fake video calls, perfect-English messages. The defence doesn't change. 3 rules:

1. Verify on a second channel: hang up, call back on the number you saved
2. Urgency is the trick: real people can wait 5 minutes while you check
3. Never share OTPs or PINs: banks and mobile-money services don't ask for them

Send this to someone who'd panic first and check later.

#scamalert #AIsafety #cybersecurity
```
