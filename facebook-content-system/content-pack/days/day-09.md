## 13:30 · Reel · D09-R1: Make AI disagree with you

**Series:** Steal This Prompt · **Content type:** Practical AI · **Format:** Talking head + screen split · **Length:** 32 s

- **The problem it solves:** Making decisions with an AI that tends to agree with whatever you suggest.
- **The idea:** AI tends to agree with the user; asking it to argue against your plan surfaces risks you missed.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** AI loved my plan. That worried me.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** Why is agreement a problem, and how do you get honest pushback?
- **Where it pays off:** 11–25.5 s: the flipped prompt surfaces risks the creator hadn't considered.
- **What they leave with:** Ask AI for the strongest reasons your plan could fail, then ask what would make it work.
- **Call to action:** Question to the viewer: Ask what decision viewers would run this on.
- **Overall look:** Chat showing 'Great idea!' → flipped prompt → list of failure reasons with two highlighted.

**Video script** (times in seconds; say the voiceover word for word)

| Time | Voiceover (say) | On-screen text | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | AI loved my plan. That worried me. | AI loved my plan. Uh-oh. | Asif; chat screen with 'Great idea!' response. |
| 3-7.5 | Ask “Is my plan good?” and it usually says yes. | “Great idea!” 🙄 | Highlight 'Great idea!' in yellow. |
| 7.5-11 | So flip the question. Make it argue against you. | Make it argue | Asif flips his hand; whoosh. |
| 11-16 | “Give me the three strongest reasons this plan could fail.” | “3 strongest reasons it fails” | Prompt typed in large text. |
| 16-21 | My plan: raise my price to 1,500 taka. Here's what came back. | Plan: Tk 1,200 → 1,500 | Screen: AI answer appears as a list. |
| 21-25.5 | Two of these I hadn't thought about. That's the point. | Risks I missed | Two items highlighted. |

| 29-32 | You decide. AI just tests the idea. | You decide | Asif to camera; end. |

**Why people keep watching**
- Hook: A personal twist (praise as a warning sign) that viewers don't expect.
- Novelty: Using AI as a critic instead of a cheerleader is new to most viewers.
- Relatability: A price increase decision is something every seller faces.
- Payoff: Two real risks the creator hadn't considered appear on screen.
- Usefulness: Two short prompts that work in any AI tool, useful before any decision.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
AI loved my plan. That worried me. Ask “Is my plan good?” and you'll usually hear yes.

Flip it:
“Give me the 3 strongest reasons this plan could fail. Be specific to my situation: [details].”

Then:
“What would make this plan work?”

I tried it on a price increase (Tk 1,200 → 1,500) and got two risks I hadn't considered.

You still decide. AI just pressure-tests the idea.

What decision would you run this on?

#AItips #decisionmaking #smallbusiness
```

- **The idea:** In health, money transfers, legal papers and people decisions, AI should prepare you, not decide for you.
- **What they leave with:** In high-stakes areas, use AI to prepare your questions and understanding, not to make the decision.
- **Call to action:** Share: Send it to someone who asks AI about everything.

**Text on the image** (use exactly)

- Headline: Use AI to prepare, not to decide, these 4
- Body:
  - Health: use it to prepare questions for your doctor.
  - Money transfers: never act on an AI or a voice you can't verify.
  - Legal papers: use it to understand terms, then ask a lawyer.
  - Hiring and firing: it can sort information, not judge people.
- Footer: AI is great at drafts and questions. You own the decision.

**What the image shows:** Four quadrants with icons (heart, money, document, people) and a 'prepare ✓ decide ✗' tag.

```text
Money transfers: never act on an AI instruction or a voice you can't verify.
```
