## 13:30 · Reel · D13-R1: Make AI your tutor, not your answer key

**Series:** Steal This Prompt · **Content type:** Practical AI · **Format:** Talking head + screen split · **Length:** 36 s

- **The problem it solves:** Students copy AI answers, then freeze in exams where there is no AI.
- **The idea:** Ask AI to give one hint at a time and ask you the next step, so you build understanding instead of copying answers.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** AI can do your homework. That's the problem.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** How do you use AI and still learn?
- **Where it pays off:** 11.5–32 s: the hint-by-hint tutor prompt in action.
- **What they leave with:** Ask AI for one hint at a time and make it ask you for the next step.
- **Call to action:** Share: Send it to a student who copies AI answers.
- **Overall look:** Student POV: full AI solution → tutor prompt → back-and-forth chat with hints.

**Why people keep watching**
- Hook: Flips the usual 'AI helps homework' message into a problem, which is surprising and slightly provocative.
- Relatability: Students (and parents) recognise copying AI answers.
- Problem → solution: Shows the problem (no learning) then the exact fix prompt.
- Usefulness: One prompt, any subject, any AI tool.
- Saves and shares: Parents and teachers will forward this to students.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
AI can do your homework. That's the problem. In the exam hall, there's no AI.

Change the deal with one prompt:
“Be my tutor. Don't give me the answer. Give me one hint at a time, then ask me what I think the next step is. If I'm wrong, explain why.”

Works for maths, physics, accounting, coding. Any subject, any AI app.

Slower, yes. But the answer ends up in your head.

Send this to a student who copies AI answers.

#students #studytips #AItips
```
