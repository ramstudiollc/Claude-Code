## 13:30 · Reel · D28-R1: Make AI grade its own answer against your checklist

**Series:** Before → After · **Content type:** Practical AI · **Format:** Before/after split screen · **Length:** 36 s

- **The problem it solves:** AI drafts that look fine but miss the details your customers always ask about.
- **The idea:** Write a short checklist of what 'good' means; ask AI to grade its draft pass/fail per rule, then rewrite to pass all.
- **Hook (first 3 s):** Looked fine. Missed the delivery time.
- **What the viewer wants to know:** How do you make AI catch its own gaps?
- **Where it pays off:** 12.5–31 s: pass/fail grading reveals two misses, then a rewrite passes every rule.
- **What they leave with:** Write a short checklist of what 'good' means and make AI grade and fix its own draft against it.
- **Call to action:** Save: Save it.
- **Overall look:** Draft → checklist → grading table with two red rows → rewritten draft with all green.

**Video script** (times in seconds; say the voiceover word for word)

| Time | Voiceover (say) | On-screen text | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Looked fine. Missed the delivery time. | Looked fine. Missed something. | A product description draft; the missing delivery time shows as an empty, highlighted gap. |
| 3-7.5 | AI's first drafts often skip what you care about. | Does it follow your rules? | Draft zoom. |
| 7.5-12.5 | Write a short checklist. Price. Delivery time. Size. Under 80 words. | Your checklist | Checklist typed. |
| 12.5-17.5 | Then: “Grade your answer against this checklist. Pass or fail, each rule.” | “Pass or fail, each rule.” | Prompt typed. |
| 17.5-22 | Look. It failed two: no delivery time, and too long. | ✗ delivery · ✗ length | Grading table with two red rows. |
| 22-26.5 | Then: “Rewrite it so every rule passes.” | “Rewrite to pass all.” | Prompt typed. |
| 26.5-31 | Now it's all green. And you didn't write a word. | All ✓ | All-green table + new draft. |
| 31-36 | Write the checklist once. Reuse it every time. | Reuse your checklist | End card. |

**Why people keep watching**
- Hook: A specific miss (the delivery time) that sellers know triggers the same customer question again and again.
- Visual progression: The grading table turns from two red rows to all green.
- Payoff: The fixed version passes every rule, a clear before/after.
- Usefulness: The checklist idea applies to product descriptions, emails, posts and reports.
- Saves and shares: Viewers save the method and their own checklist.

**Caption** (copy and paste)

```text
The AI draft looked fine. It missed the delivery time. Make AI grade its own answer:

1. Write a short checklist of what “good” means. For a product description: price, delivery time, size, under 80 words, no exaggerated claims.
2. “Grade your answer against this checklist. Pass or fail for each rule.”
3. “Rewrite it so every rule passes.”

The checklist is the secret. Write it once and reuse it for every product, email or post.

#AItips #ChatGPT #productivity
```

**Notes:** Record the real run; if the first draft fails different rules, update the VO in beat 5 to match.
