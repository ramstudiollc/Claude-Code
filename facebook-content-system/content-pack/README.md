# Content pack: the 30-day plan without the design

Everything to **say and show** for 30 days of Facebook posts, as plain text: the idea, the plan, every Reel script and every image's text and caption. Design (colours, fonts, templates) is left to your own brand guidelines.

## The idea

> **"I build automations for real businesses, and I'll show you the useful parts for free: the exact prompt, the exact workflow, and what it can't do."**

The page becomes the place people go for practical AI and n8n automation they can use the same day. Most posts teach for free; a smaller share shows your real builds and process, so business owners who want it done for them message you. Audience, in order: (1) F-commerce and small-business owners (your n8n buyers), (2) freelancers and young professionals (reach and shares), (3) students (saves).

## What gets posted

- **120 posts in 30 days** (Mon 12 Oct → Tue 10 Nov 2026): **60 Reels + 60 images**, every day:
  - 09:30 image · 13:30 Reel · 18:30 image · 21:00 Reel (Bangladesh time)
- **Reels:** 30–51 s (median 43 s), voiced by you, captions on screen, one clear idea each.
- **Images:** single 4:5 images, one idea each, 75 words or fewer on the image.

## Writing rules (already applied; keep them if you edit)

1. Outcome first: the first sentence says what the viewer gets.
2. Plain English for second-language readers: short sentences, no idioms or slang.
3. Specific beats general: name the button, paste the prompt, count the steps.
4. Say the limits: who can use it and what it can't do.
5. First person, calm and practical. No shouting, no fake urgency, no engagement bait, no links in the post text.
6. No invented numbers or clients. Example figures are labelled as examples.

## Before you post

- A few posts contain personal statements ("I use…", "mine was…"). Keep them only if they are true for you; each one is flagged in that post's **Notes**.
- Posts with ⚠️ sources mention fast-changing facts (prices, features, rules). Re-check those within 48 hours of posting.
- Notes that mention `design-system.md` or a template refer to the original plan's design files. Your own brand guidelines replace them.
